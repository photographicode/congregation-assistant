A few good questions.

Is this an organization-run application?

No. Congregation Assistant is independent and unofficial. It does not use JW logos and is not endorsed by the organization.

Can publishers use a shared email?

Yes, when the administrator explicitly confirms household reporting. Reporting access does not share another person’s contact details or availability.

Is Private mode available now?

No. The cryptographic foundation is under development. Full device approval, recovery, revocation, migration and sentinel verification must pass before release.

Do we need an Android app store account?

No. Install the progressive web app from your browser for free. A signed APK is optional and requires signing configuration. Google Play publication is a separate owner action.

Are meeting titles imported automatically?

Not yet. The importer remains disabled until written authorization and the centralized data pipeline are verified. Watchtower titles are entered and reviewed by your meeting overseer.

What can we try today?

Explore the fictional admin or publisher demo. No real records are used, and demo changes never reach the congregation database.
