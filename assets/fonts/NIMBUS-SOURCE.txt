Nimbus Sans Embedded TrueType fonts are conversions made 14 October 2026 from URW Nimbus Sans original OTF outlines. The converter renames the derived family and preserves metrics. Original outlines, full license and conversion source: NIMBUS-SOURCE.zip. Source project: https://github.com/ArtifexSoftware/urw-base35-fonts .
