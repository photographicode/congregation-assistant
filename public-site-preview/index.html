A few useful answers

Before you
get started.

Clear expectations make getting started easier.

Can older users use it comfortably?

The mobile workspace uses larger controls, readable text, and sections that match desktop. Try it with the people who will use it; feedback during your trial helps identify confusing steps.

Do I need to download anything?

You can open the application in a modern browser. Once opened, use Install app when your browser offers it, or add it to your home screen. An internet connection is needed for cloud records and publishing.

How does the meeting live link work?

After cloud setup, the overseer publishes the schedule and shares its stable link. Later published changes update that same link. Draft edits remain private until published. Older snapshot links need to be replaced with the new live link.

Who can access congregation records?

Your administrator approves Google accounts and assigns roles during setup. Private-record access must be enforced by the configured database. Public report, attendance, and schedule links are intended for their recipients and should be shared carefully.

What happens after the 30-day trial?

You decide whether to continue. The introductory annual offer is ₹1,499 per congregation. Payment and activation are arranged manually; this website does not collect a card or charge you automatically.

Can we keep our own backup?

You can export supported contact and report records. Those files are useful offline copies. They are not a complete database backup; full backup and recovery arrangements should be confirmed during setup.

Is this an official application?

Congregation Assistant is an independently developed application. Using familiar report names does not imply official endorsement. Confirm your congregation's authorization before submitting an account request.
