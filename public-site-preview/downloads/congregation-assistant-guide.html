2 / What it does

A place for each task.

Choose the tools your congregation needs. You do not have to use every feature at once.

Publisher records & groups

Manage names, contact details, appointments, qualifications, emergency contacts and field service groups. Transfer is an occasional administrator action.

Monthly field service reports

Publishers submit the familiar standalone form. Administrators review submissions, correct records and prepare publisher record card publisher records.

Meeting attendance

Record attendance for the configured meeting calendar. Review history and prepare monthly and yearly attendance summaries documents.

Meeting scheduling / OCLM

Choose a week, assign people, review, publish and check the live schedule. Qualification, availability and assignment history help selection.

Additional duties

Audio Video, Attendant and Cleaning overseers manage their own assignments and publish independently. Custom duties accommodate local needs.

Personal publisher pages

See upcoming assignments and the schedule, submit reports, set away dates, maintain contact details and create reminders.

Administration and support

The service operator manages congregation account requests and service administration. Congregation administrators control local records and access. Those are different responsibilities.

5 / Reports

Submit once.
Check the confirmation.

The personal page uses the familiar standalone field service report form.

  1. Open Report. If family reporting is approved, select the correct publisher.
  2. Check the reporting month. Complete ministry participation, Bible studies, hours when applicable and comments.
  3. Check the fields shown. Pioneer options and required hours depend on the approved publisher record.
  4. Submit and wait for “saved online”. The confirmation tells you that another submission is not needed.
  5. Correct a saved report only when necessary. Use the available correction action; avoid repeated submissions.
If a save fails

Keep the form open. Your entered values should remain available. Check your connection and retry; do not assume the report reached the congregation until the online confirmation appears.

Service-year records

Field service reports keep the current service year and the previous service year as complete years for publisher record card printing. When the service year changes in September, older complete years are removed by scheduled cleanup. Restoration excludes expired reports. Downloaded backups need separate removal; hosting-provider backup cycles may retain deleted data temporarily.

An administrator reviews reports and corrects appointments. A regular pioneer’s low-hours indication is a review aid; infirm status is taken into account.

7 / Meeting schedules

One week.
Five clear steps.

The OCLM overseer handles meeting assignments; additional-duty overseers handle their own work.

  1. Choose the week. Confirm the meeting date and the S-140 meeting parts.
  2. Assign people. Use the congregation publisher list and the qualification, availability and recent-assignment information. Whole-week automatic assignment is a starting point for review.
  3. Review section by section. Check names, assistants, titles and unassigned parts. Adjust any unsuitable choice.
  4. Publish the meeting schedule. You do not have to wait for Audio Video, Attendant or Cleaning duties.
  5. Check the live schedule. Open the published notice board, select the week and search for a fictional test name.
Saved is not the same as published

A device draft is held in that browser. Saved online means the authorized workspace has accepted it. Published means the shareable schedule has been updated. Wait for the relevant confirmation before relying on a change.

8 / Shared notice board

Different overseers.
The same notice board.

Each department publishes when its own work is ready.

Audio Video

Use custom duty names such as Audio, Video, Platform or Microphone. Request eligible publishers from the congregation list.

Attendant

Assign the meeting attendant and any custom sub-duties. Attendance recording remains a separate task.

Cleaning

Schedule cleaning responsibilities in the department workspace. Use congregation publishers and the current approval workflow.

Administrator approvals

The main administrator reviews department publisher requests. Approved publishers become available to that department; overseers cannot add outsiders.

  1. Request the publishers you need. Choose from the existing congregation list.
  2. Wait for administrator approval. Approval requests appear in the administrator notification centre.
  3. Assign the week’s duties. Check the correct duty, date and person.
  4. Publish your department. Its published assignments appear on the same OCLM notice board without republishing other departments.
What if a department has not finished?

The meeting schedule can still be published. Unassigned additional duties show “Not assigned”. Name search includes published meeting assignments and departmental duties.

9 / Attendance & records

Keep routine work short.

Use the matching workspace rather than searching through unrelated settings.

  1. For attendance, open Attendance. Check meeting type and date; enter the count and save.
  2. Review the month. Check totals before creating monthly attendance record or yearly attendance summary documents. Memorial records have their own history.
  3. For publisher details, open Publishers. Search the name and edit the authorized fields. Check the saved confirmation.
  4. For groups, open Groups. Review membership and overseer assignments. Transferred publishers should not remain eligible.
  5. For a transfer, use the secondary publisher action. Review the contact-information document and confirm the transfer carefully. Access and assignment eligibility are revoked.
Check documents before sharing

Confirm names, dates, service year and totals in the generated PDF. Preview or print a fictional example on the intended printer. Automated layout checks cannot guarantee every printer or every possible data length.

Deletion is an occasional action

Review the publisher name and the warning before confirming deletion. Ask the administrator when unsure; avoid deleting a record just to remove one assignment.

10 / Copies & recovery

A backup you can find.
A password you can keep.

Only the main administrator restores the supported congregation workspace.

  1. Make a full backup. Open Settings → backup and recovery → Make backup. Choose a password of at least 12 characters.
  2. Check the downloaded file. Store it securely. Keep its password separately; support cannot recover a forgotten backup password.
  3. To restore, choose the encrypted backup. Enter its password and review the publisher and report counts before continuing.
  4. Keep a safety copy of today’s records. The restore flow downloads one before replacing the reviewed workspace.
  5. Restore online and reload. Check the restored records, schedule and access on an authorized device.
A full backup versus CSV

A full encrypted workspace backup contains supported records, settings and schedules. CSV spreadsheets are unencrypted. In Settings, download a fictional sample, replace its values, and choose “Import publishers and reports together”. Check the summary before confirming. Contacts and reports save together; unrelated records stay in place. CSV does not replace a full backup or approve publisher login.

If restoration fails

Retain the original backup file and password. Check the error and connection, then review again. A conflict means records changed online; do not force an old snapshot over new work. Contact support with the error text, never the publisher data or password.

Private links and accounts

Restoration does not replace billing, administrator/department accounts or live-link tokens. Publisher access for omitted records is removed. Restoring a publisher later does not automatically reapprove their account.

11 / Privacy & reminders

Know what is shared.

Use the least access needed and explain it before entering publisher information.

Who developed it?

Congregation Assistant is independently developed by a brother based in India who serves full-time in Jehovah’s organisation. It is not an official application of Jehovah’s Witnesses and is not endorsed by their organisation.

Who can access private records?

Approved accounts receive role-based access checked by the secure backend. Congregation administrators manage local access. Authorized service administrators and backend operators have privileged access to stored records. This is not end-to-end encryption.

Is it end-to-end encrypted?

No. Password-protected backup exports use encryption, but that does not make the online database end-to-end encrypted. A domain purchase does not change this.

Are shared links private sign-in pages?

A recipient who has a schedule or form link may be able to use it without publisher sign-in. Treat links as access credentials. Administrators can revoke or replace supported links.

Will reminders arrive when the app is closed?

Calendar exports provide device-calendar reminders. In-app reminders work while the app is open. Background push and automatic email remain prepared but inactive until sender setup and delivery testing are complete.

Where is information hosted?

The current interface and website use GitHub Pages; the secure workspace uses Supabase. Your browser also stores interface preferences and supported local drafts. Secure your device and sign out on shared phones.

Trust requires checks

Confirm congregation authorization, review the current privacy explanation and test approved access before importing real records. Separate Privacy Policy and Terms wording remains under review; this guide is not a substitute for those documents.

12 / Common questions

Answers before you start.

Use the online guide for current screenshots and detailed steps.

Can an older person use large text?

The app is designed for larger text and comfortable touch controls. Test the actual phone with its font and display settings, including the report form and schedule. Ask users where they hesitate.

Must everyone install an app?

No. Open the website in a supported browser. Android browsers may offer Install; iPhone/iPad users normally use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Browser support varies; no web app can guarantee installation or push on every device/version.

What about a weak connection?

Internet is needed for approved sign-in, cloud records and publication. A visible draft is not proof of an online save. Keep entered work and retry when connected; wait for the saved confirmation.

Do we need mobile OTP or a new password?

Approved publisher access uses Google sign-in. Shared-family report access can be explicitly approved by the administrator. Phone OTP is not advertised as an active login option.

What does it cost?

The website currently describes a 30-day trial and ₹1,499 introductory annual offer per congregation. Payment/activation is manual. Confirm the current offer and cancellation/refund arrangements before payment; no automatic card charge occurs on the website.

Can we suggest changes?

Yes. Send feedback to . Describe the task, device/browser, expected result and what happened. Use fictional examples; do not send private links, credentials or publisher records.
