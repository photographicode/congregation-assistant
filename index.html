About Congregation Assistant

A connected workspace for congregation records, monthly reporting and service oversight.

Publishers — the central database

Store publisher profiles, service assignments, household relationships, group membership and monthly service information in one place. Publisher filters can be combined with month and service-year views, and filtered results can be downloaded as publisher record card cards.

Groups and publisher reporting

Groups provide focused rosters, group-level reporting and a dedicated form-link workflow. Group overseers can receive the appropriate link so publishers can submit their monthly information without exposing administrative controls.

Home and Analytics

Home brings together submission status, service totals and important congregation indicators. Analytics provides broader roster and service summaries from the information already stored in the database.

Forms and downloads

Print-ready publisher record cards and attendance summaries use original layouts and bundled Noto Sans. These are independent administrative exports, not independent record exports.

Settings and data portability

Settings contains account controls, subscription information, application appearance, font selection, data import/export and the data-management tools available to the current administrator.

Accessibility and appearance

The application uses Noto Sans for clear, consistent text and offers interface appearance choices. Theme colors are intended for the interface only, with explicit contrast and focus treatment for interactive controls.

Cloud synchronization

When cloud mode is configured, congregation records, publishers, reports and enabled modules are synchronized with the connected database. The sync indicator communicates the current application state.

Subscription and access

Trial and active status control access to the application. An expired subscription is locked until an authorized administrator restores an eligible status.
