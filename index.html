About Congregation Assistant

A connected workspace for congregation records, monthly reporting and service oversight.

Publishers — the central database

Store publisher profiles, service assignments, household relationships, group membership and monthly service information in one place. Publisher filters can be combined with month and service-year views, and filtered results can be downloaded as S-21 cards.

Groups and publisher reporting

Groups provide focused rosters, group-level reporting and a dedicated form-link workflow. Group overseers can receive the appropriate link so publishers can submit their monthly information without exposing administrative controls.

Home and Analytics

Home brings together submission status, service totals and important congregation indicators. Analytics provides broader roster and service summaries from the information already stored in the database.

Forms and downloads

S-21, S-3-E and S-88-E are generated from their supplied form templates. Application theme settings do not alter the appearance of those official forms.

Settings and data portability

Settings contains account controls, subscription information, application appearance, font selection, data import/export and the data-management tools available to the current administrator.

Accessibility and appearance

The application provides four interface themes and a Noto Sans/Poppins font choice. Theme colors are intended for the interface only, with explicit contrast and focus treatment for interactive controls.

Cloud synchronization

When cloud mode is configured, congregation records, publishers, reports and enabled modules are synchronized with the connected database. The sync indicator communicates the current application state.

Subscription and access

Trial and active status control access to the application. An expired subscription is locked until an authorized administrator restores an eligible status.

How to use Congregation Assistant

A quick walkthrough from setup to daily reporting.

1. Add a publisher

Open Publishers, choose Add Publisher, enter the person's details and save. The new record is then available to reports, groups and analytics.

2. Record monthly reports

From Home, use the monthly submissions area to review reports. Missing reports can be identified and reminders can be sent.

3. Use Groups & Analytics

Groups keeps people organised, while Analytics provides roster and service summaries.

4. Attendance & Emergency Contacts

When enabled for your congregation, these modules appear automatically in the navigation.

Demo congregation

The demo congregation follows the same empty-database behavior as a normal congregation. Add publishers and records to build its data.
