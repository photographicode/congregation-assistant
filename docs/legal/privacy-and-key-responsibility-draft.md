# Terms / privacy / key responsibility — needs lawyer review

Draft, not a final legal policy.

The current service uses account controls and server-stored records; authorized service operators can technically access that data. Congregation administrators must have an appropriate basis and authorization to provide records and manage access. A reviewed policy must specify purposes, processors, retention, rights and support channels.

Planned Private mode encrypts covered data on the device. The user controls keys and recovery material. If all key holders lose their keys and recovery phrase, covered data is permanently unrecoverable and the provider cannot restore it. Revocation does not recall already obtained copies. Metadata, login emails, compromised devices and user-shared exports are outside the encrypted scope. Old backups may retain plaintext for their retention period. No zero-risk guarantee is made.

WhatsApp delivery requires clear, withdrawable consent; optional AI intake sends message content through WhatsApp and processing servers and is disabled in Private mode. Implement and review consent/opt-out, DPDP-style responsibilities and retention before activation. Billing, refunds, jurisdiction, liability and processor terms require legal review.
