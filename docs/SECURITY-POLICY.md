# Security reports

Report privately to security@congregationassistant.com after the Titan mailbox is configured. Include URL, timestamp, browser, reproduction steps and impact. Do not email live congregation records or credentials. Keep demonstrations limited to fictional data and accounts you control.

Internal response targets: acknowledge within three business days, triage urgent account/data exposure within 24 hours of receipt, communicate material findings and remediation progress. These are operational goals; email delivery and staffing must be verified before public response-time commitments.

No independent security audit has been completed. Encryption lifecycle claims stay disabled until device approval, recovery, migration, revocation and no-plaintext sentinel suites all pass. Refer to the actual permission tests and reports, not a count inferred from source inspection.
