# How your data is protected

Current mode is Standard: HTTPS, account approval and server access controls. The service operator can technically access stored records. Do not expect end-to-end encryption in the preview.

Private mode is planned and its cryptographic foundation is being tested. Before it is offered, it must support verified recovery, device approval, revocation, a tested encrypted backup and a complete data migration that checks every ciphertext before removing plaintext.

When it is released, save your recovery phrase offline and consider a second approved administrator as a custodian. If every key holder loses their keys and the recovery phrase, covered records are permanently unrecoverable. The service cannot recover those keys for you. Removing access cannot recall copies someone already obtained.

Even in Private mode, emails used for login and limited metadata remain visible. Messages you choose to send through WhatsApp, printed records and exported files have their own privacy limits. Protect the device and verify safety codes with other administrators outside the app when a key changes.
