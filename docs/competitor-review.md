# Competitor review — primary sources checked 5 October 2026

| Product | Observed strengths | Implication for Congregation Assistant |
|---|---|---|
| [Organized](https://about.organized-app.com/) | Free, modern web UI, offline features, roles, themes, schedules, availability, reports, public-talk collaboration and territories | Strongest broad free comparison. Charge for dependable simplicity/support rather than unsupported superiority claims. Its import feature does not establish permission to redistribute JW content. |
| [NW Scheduler / NW Publisher](https://nwscheduler.com/jw-scheduler-publisher-edition-ios-android-app/) | Desktop administration plus free publisher companion app; assignments, reports and many congregation workflows | Native delivery and assignment acceptance are gaps for us. Compare [registration terms](https://nwscheduler.com/register-jw-scheduler/) directly; do not invent a price. |
| [Hourglass](https://www.hourglass-app.com/en/features/) | Service reports, S-21, reminders and publisher-oriented mobile workflows | Reliable reminders and quick report collection are expectations, not unique features. |
| [Territory Helper](https://www.territoryhelper.com/Help) | Specialized territory mapping/management and companion tools | Integrate later only with a separate privacy model for territory data; do not dilute the scheduling/report MVP. |

Other products worth a separately verified comparison: KHS, CLM Explorer, TsWin, Eutaktos and Unitae. 'Zeal' has not been reliably identified from the user's description, so no product-specific claims are made. This is a representative comparison, not an exhaustive list of every application worldwide.

Immediate paid value: approachable setup, role-specific workspaces, clear failure recovery, readable mobile controls, synchronized limited roster, searchable S-140-style publication and configurable additional duties. Next priorities: shared drafts and conflict detection, availability, assignment acknowledgment, weekend talks, accessible multilingual exports, full recovery drills and responsive support. Make free trial outcomes measurable: time to first successful report/schedule, number of failed saves, support requests, and whether users can complete tasks without an explanation.
